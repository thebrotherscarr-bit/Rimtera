**Master plan:** [ROADMAP_0.2.0.md](../ROADMAP_0.2.0.md)

- 0.1.11: world zones; the vault and canopy, used by the Colossus and Flora arenas

**IDs (19):** BO-3, BO-4, BO-19, BO-20, BO-12, BO-13, IC-8, IC-9, IC-12, IC-16, IC-20, PU-18, IC-22, PU-19, PU-20, PC-11, PE-3, WT-16, WT-18.

**Non-ID:** X-CP-4.

## Goals

- **The analogs of Terraria's structurally special bosses are built that way (D-10):**
  - worm bosses: the Fallout Hive-mind, which may split, and the Cyber-Swarm Leader, which launches drones from its segments;
  - multi-part bosses: the Scout Mech (2 hands), Renegade Overseer (4 arms), Warship Core (twin cores), Vault Colossus (2 fists and a detachable head), Pirate Dreadnought and Mothership Core (turrets);
  - the Fission Tyrant as a moving wall with a tether;
  - the Astro-God, with hands and a head before its core.
- **Boss loot:**
  - every boss has ≥ 2 pool items, including the Fission Tyrant's class-emblem analog;
  - each of the 33 core-chips has a unique effect (D-17).
- **Gear depth:**
  - minion behaviours (contact, orbit shooter, dash striker), line of sight, alt-use targeting and slot costs;
  - whip tags;
  - projectile behaviours: arrow gravity, bounce, split, chain, sticky, sword beams, flail spin;
  - Terraria's explosives rules (D-38);
  - the missing accessory families;
  - T5-T7 set abilities through the double-tap set-bonus input (X-CP-4).
- **Inventory parity:**
  - 4 coin and 4 ammo slots;
  - the equipment page with dye slots, vanity accessories, hide toggles and misc slots (pet, light pet, mount, hook);
  - 3 more banks and a void-bag analog;
  - accessory slots 6 and 7 (D-8);
  - worn armor and vanity drawn on the player (D-56).
- **Building depth:**
  - wiring: a 4th wire, sensors, pumps, a junction box and traps;
  - paint for tiles and walls (D-34).

## Stream A: Segmented and multi-part bosses

### A1 · BO-4: multi-part bosses
- **Part model.** A parent/child model: `BossPartRow` and `EnemyAgent.ParentId`.
  - Each part has its own HP and patterns.
  - The parent is invulnerable until its required parts are dead.
- **Bars.** Parts register with BO-10's aggregate bar (0.1.10), so the bar is the sum of the parts.
- **Saves.** Part links survive save and load. Bosses are dropped on quit (BO-17), so this matters only for autosave mid-fight within a session; the snapshot round-trip must still hold.
- **Targets (D-10):**
  - Scout Mech: 2 hands.
  - Renegade Overseer: 4 arms (the Skeletron Prime shape).
  - Warship Core: 2 linked bodies (the Twins shape).
  - Vault Colossus: 2 fists and a detachable head.
  - Pirate Dreadnought and Mothership Core: turrets.
- **Tests (`BossPartsSelfTests`):**
  - The Scout Mech's body takes 0 damage while either hand lives.
  - The Warship Core's twins share phase logic.
  - Part links round-trip through a snapshot.

### A2 · BO-3: worm bosses
- **Segments.** Segmented bosses share HP in the `realLife` style. Segment code is reused from the `Worm` family.
- **Fallout Hive-mind** (the EoW analog): it may split into independent worms when a mid segment dies.
- **Cyber-Swarm Leader** (the Destroyer analog): segments launch drones.
- Both burrow through tiles (BO-1's Burrow pattern).
- Segment and tail sprites use aliases until art.
- **Tests:**
  - ≥ 20 segments.
  - Damage to any segment lowers the shared bar.
  - It moves through solid tiles.
  - The kill drops loot once.
  - The Hive-mind split yields ≥ 2 worms.

### A3 · BO-19: the Fission Tyrant as a wall
- **The wall.** A full-height moving barrier that advances steadily toward one side of the world from where it was summoned, at ≥ 1 tile/s.
- **Weak points.** Its weak points are BO-4 parts and fire beams.
- **The tether.** It pulls a player who gets more than 40 tiles ahead.
- **Player death.** It resets rather than leaving.
- **Tests:**
  - It moves ≥ 1 tile/s in one direction for 20 s regardless of the player.
  - The tether pulls a player who is more than 40 tiles ahead.
  - Summoning it outside the Irradiated Core is still refused (0.1.10 refusal table).

### A4 · BO-20: Astro-God phases
- **Parts.** Hands and a head (BO-4), with beam, sphere and leech patterns.
- **The core.** It is shielded until both hands and the head die.
- **Messages.** A phase-change message appears for each part destroyed.
- **Tests:**
  - The core takes 0 damage until all three parts die, then takes damage.
  - Each phase message fires.
  - The bar drains through the parts.

## Stream B: Boss loot

- Add 2-4 tier-appropriate items each for the Fission Tyrant, Void Cantor and Fallout Hive-mind.

  - the Overseer's dash;
  - the Alpha Skitter's fall immunity and bounce;
  - the Hive-mind's on-hit spore cloud;
  - the Fission Tyrant's +1 accessory slot (Brutal+ only, matching D-8);
  - the Astro-God's gravity control.

- **Test:** `--item-audit` lists a distinct effect tag per chip, and no two chips have identical stats.

## Stream C: Weapons and minions

### C1 · IC-8: minion behaviour variety and targeting
- **Behaviours.** Add `MinionSpec.Behaviour`: ContactChaser, OrbitShooter (today's behaviour) and DashStriker.
- **Line of sight.** Shooters need line of sight.
- **Targeting.** Alt-use (right-click) on a creature sets the minion target, through the Terraria-style binding added in controls phase 2's input layer.
- **Slot cost.** Each kind has its own slot cost.
- **Tests:**
  - A contact minion damages only on touch.
  - Shooters do not fire through solid tiles.
  - The alt-use target is preferred over the nearest one.
  - A 2-slot minion uses 2 slots.

### C2 · IC-9: whip tags per whip
- **Tags.** `ItemDefinition.TagDamage` (flat damage added to minion hits on the tagged target for 4 s) and an optional `TagCrit`.
- **Speed.** Whip use time scales with a summon attack-speed stat.
- **The melee "lash".** It becomes a Command whip (`ItemSystem.cs` field append, `WeaponStyleAssignments.cs` ≈68, `WeaponStyles.cs` ≈313, `Combat.cs` ≈424).
- **Tests:**
  - TagDamage 5 adds exactly 5 (before defense) to each minion hit on the tagged creature.
  - Every whip reports the Command class.

### C3 · IC-12: the projectile behaviour library (D-38)
- **Flags on `ProjectileState`:**
  - gravity for arrows (lighter than thrown items);
  - bounce;
  - split on hit;
  - chain to the nearest target;
  - sticky, then explode later.
- **Sword beams.** Broadswords with `ProjectileSpeed > 0` fire one beam per swing.
- **Flails.** Hold to spin, release to launch.
- **Explosives (D-38):**
  - Bombs and dynamite hurt the player and break tiles, respecting `IsExplosionProof`.
  - Most rockets do neither, except the destructive variants.
  - Explosions respect the temple and dungeon protection, as Terraria's protect the Lihzahrd and dungeon bricks.
- **Tests (one per behaviour):**
  - An arrow's apex lies below its straight line.
  - A bouncer reflects off a wall.
  - A splitter spawns N children.
  - A chain hits 3 creatures.
  - A bomb damages its thrower and breaks dirt but not `PrecursorBrick`.

- **Tags and items:**

  - longer immunity (`immune:+0.67`);
  - an on-hurt star or bee burst;

  - a dash on double-tap, using the movement code from 0.1.8;

  - charge on hurt;
  - yo-yo string and counterweight;
  - arrow, bullet and rocket multipliers;
  - the potion-sickness reducer, if IC-2 had to defer it;
  - an Ankh-like debuff-immunity combiner, built from IC-3's immunity accessories.

- **Audit.** Extend `ItemAudit` known-tags.
- **Tests:**
  - `--item-audit` shows 0 unread tags.
  - One self-test per tag.

### D2 · IC-20 and X-CP-4: signature set abilities
- **Abilities.** T5-T7 sets each get one active or visible ability, triggered by Terraria's set-bonus input (double-tap down, added in the controls layer):
  - a dash or a shield;
  - a guardian minion;
  - stealth while standing still;
  - heal bolts **for colonists only**. Decision 3: never for the player.
- They sit alongside the existing 8 specials.
- **Tests:**
  - One test per ability.
  - The heal-bolt set never restores the player's HP.

## Stream E: Inventory, equipment, armor visuals

### E1 · PU-18 and IC-22: coin and ammo slots
- **Slots.** 4 coin and 4 ammo slots outside the 50-slot grid.
- **Ammo.** `FindAmmo` checks the ammo slots first.
- **Credits.** Credits fill the coin slots on pickup.
- **Saves.** One character-format bump (the PU-11 hook) covers E1-E4.
- **Tests:**
  - Ammo in slot A1 and in the grid: A1 is spent first.
  - Coins land in the coin slots.
  - A 0.1.13 `.rchar` loads.

### E2 · PU-19: the equipment page
- **Dye slots.** One dye slot per armor and accessory slot. It holds the dye item and does not consume it. Old consumed dyes migrate into the dye slots.
- **Vanity.** A vanity slot beside each accessory.
- **Hide toggles.**
- **Misc page.** Pet, light pet, mount and hook slots. Equipping summons the pet or pet light. QuickMount and QuickGrapple use these slots.
- **Tests:**
  - A dye recolours its piece, and removing it returns the dye.
  - A hidden accessory keeps its stats but is not drawn.
  - The pet slot summons and despawns the pet.

### E3 · PU-20: more personal banks
- **Banks.** Up to 3 more 40-slot banks, reached from new station items: the safe, forge-vault and void-vault analogs.
- **Void bag.** A void-bag analog auto-deposits when the grid is full.
- **Tests:**
  - Each bank keeps its contents across worlds.
  - A full grid with the void bag moves a picked-up stack into it.

### E4 · PC-11: accessory slots 6 and 7 (D-8)
- **Slot 6.** The auxiliary socket opens it only in Brutal+ worlds.
- **Slot 7.** Annihilation adds `Accessory7` (append-only enum), for a maximum of 7.
- **Drawing.** The slots are drawn in `InventoryUi`. They also respect IC-21's `Slot6Open` (0.1.9).
- **Tests:**
  - With a socket: 5 slots in a Classic world, 6 on Brutal, 7 on Annihilation.
  - An old save loads with no item loss. An item in a now-locked slot moves to the inventory.

### E5 · PE-3: worn armor and vanity drawn on the player (D-56)
- **Paper-doll layer.** One anchor-based worn sprite per piece (head, body, legs), placed by the rig anchors in `RigFor` per body frame. Vanity overrides armor.
- **Draw order.** Back accessories → legs → body → arm and held item → head → front accessories.
- **Dyes.** Per piece: a dye in the head slot tints only the helmet (`DyedLook`).
- **Art.** All 152 pieces get placeholder worn sprites, derived from the item icon with a mask. The real worn sheets are queued, and they run on the user's go. Per-frame overlays are deferred (X-PE-1).
- **Memory.** Sheets load through PE-2's lazy atlas.
- **Tests:**
  - Every `EquipmentSlot.Head/Body/Legs` item resolves to a worn-layer sprite or an explicit "no-visual" flag.
  - Play-test with `--kit arsenal`: each piece visibly changes the player.

## Stream F: Wiring and paint

### F1 · WT-16: wiring completeness
- **Wire.** A 4th wire layer.
- **Sensors.** Day/night, player-above and liquid.
- **Pumps.** Inlet and outlet pumps move liquid between their networks.
- **Junction box.**
- **Traps.** Spike and flame traps.
- **Ids.** Appended tile ids, with recipes at the Chief Engineer's shop or the Fabricator, and placeholder sprites.
- **Tests (BatchA wiring self-test):**
  - A pump pair moves water.
  - A junction box routes straight through.
  - A day sensor toggles a lamp at dawn.

### F2 · WT-18: paint (D-34)
- **Tile paint.** Uses the cell's 5 spare bits (`TileGrid.cs` ≈781), which hold 31 colours. Wall paint needs codec version 3: one extra byte per cell, written only when any wall in the chunk is painted (`CellCodec.cs`, migration from v2).
- **Items.** 31 paints from the dye materials at the Dye Vat, a paintbrush and roller, and a scraper.
- **Rendering.** The tile draw is tinted.
- **Tests:**
  - Painted cells survive a save round-trip.
  - A v2 world loads unchanged.
  - The scraper removes paint.
  - The Large-world save size grows by ≤ 5% when nothing is painted.

## Risks
- **The largest step.** Mitigation: six independent streams with sole-owner files. A and E are the critical path.
- **Three save-format changes:** character, codec and part links. Mitigation: one bump per file, PU-11 hooks and fixtures.
- **The armor art volume.** Mitigation: icon-derived placeholders. Real sheets come on the user's go.
- **Scope.** If time is short, defer WT-18 and IC-20 past 0.2.0 (master §8.4). Each cut is local.
