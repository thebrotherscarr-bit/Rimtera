# 0.1.8: Foundations (clock, statuses, movement, menus)

**Master plan:** [ROADMAP_0.2.0.md](../ROADMAP_0.2.0.md) · **Depends on:** 0.1.7 (PU-11 migration hooks, BO-6's `BoundaryPassed` helper, NC-2's player-needs removal) · **Size:** L (≈30 agent-days; 5 streams)

**IDs (23):** EV-17, EV-4, IC-1, PU-1, PU-2, PU-3, PU-5, PC-10, PU-6, PU-7, IC-6, PU-10, PC-13, PC-14, PU-12, PU-13, PU-14, PU-15, PU-16, PU-24, PU-26, PE-15, PE-16. **Non-ID:** X-CR-10.

## Goals

- **Terraria's clock.** A full day and night lasts 24 real minutes (decision 2, D-29).
- **Real time for statuses.**
  - Every status, regen and damage over time runs on real seconds (D-37).
  - Regen and DoT use Terraria's units.
  - Buffs last minutes, not seconds.
- **Events end at the right boundary.** Moons end at dawn, the Dark Sun at dusk. An event that times out is not "cleared".
- **The player moves like Terraria's** (D-40, D-41):
  - run, jump, gravity and terminal speed;
  - fall damage and breath;
  - per-mount physics;
  - flippers for swimming (D-44).
- **Respawn follows Terraria** (D-7): 10 s, ×1.5 on Brutal+, a bed spawn point, no single-player boss penalty.
- **Creative is split from normal play** (D-46):
  - research lives on the character;
  - Creative characters and worlds stay apart;
  - kit polish;
  - Journey weather and spread powers.
- **Saves, pause and menus.**
  - Autosave off the UI thread, with an interval preference (D-42).
  - Terraria pause rules.
  - The Hazard row and map fixes.
  - Buff-bar tooltips and cancel.
  - Feats and credits on the title screen (D-45).
  - Video rows.
  - Camera snap and shake options (D-58).
  - Lower audio latency.

## Stream A: Clock and statuses

### A1 · EV-17: day length matches Terraria (D-29)
- **Clock change:**
  - Keep the 06:00/18:00 boundaries.
  - Day: 12 game hours over 900 real seconds, so `GameHoursPerRealSecond = 1/75` (0.013333).
  - Night: 12 hours over 540 s, so `NightClockPace = 900/540` (1.6667).
  - Add the developer flag `--fast-clock`, which restores 0.08 and pace 1.5, for tests only.
- **Rescale every per-game-hour quantity that must stay real-time.**
  - Grep `GameHoursPerRealSecond`, `PerHour`, `Hours` in `Systems/` (`Player.cs` ≈216, 675, 765; `Phase.cs` ≈55).
  - Classify each one:
    - **Real-time** (keep the per-real-second value): statuses, regen, potion timers (moved to IC-1), the fishing bite wait, the travelling merchant's stay.
    - **Clock-time, stays per game hour** (it naturally lengthens 6× in real time): crop growth, town arrivals and `TownRespawnHours` (Terraria's are per in-game day), event durations (EV-4 also ends them at boundaries), colonist needs.
  - Check the colonist needs against D-48's invariance: hunger per **real** minute must stay within ±10% of today's. So colonist need decay is divided by 6 per game hour.
- **Event chances** stay per in-game day. With the 6× longer day, real-time frequency drops to Terraria's.
- **Tests (`ClockSelfTests`):**
  - One full cycle = 1,440 ± 10 real seconds (simulate 1,440 s at a 60 Hz tick).
  - Day 900 ± 5 s and night 540 ± 5 s.
  - A natural Rust Moon lasts about 9 real minutes (06:00 end).
  - Colonist hunger per real minute is within ±10% of 0.1.7's.
  - `--fast-clock` restores the 250 s cycle.

### A2 · IC-1: statuses and regen on real seconds (D-37)
- `StatusDefinition` stores `DurationSeconds` and regen and DoT in Terraria `lifeRegen` units (half-HP per second ×2).
- `StatusEffectController.Advance` takes real seconds, so sleep fast-forward no longer drains buffs.
- Gear `LifeRegenerationPerHour` becomes `LifeRegenUnits`, added in `UpdateLifeRegen`. This resolves GEAR_DIFF D10. Debuffs zero out positive regen (Terraria §9.1).
- **Re-author durations:**
  - utility potions 2-8 min;
  - class tonics 4 min;
  - Well Fed 5-30 min by tier;
  - potion sickness 60 s (IC-2 finishes the ladder in 0.1.9).
- **Saves:** convert old hour-based remaining durations on load (remaining real seconds = hours × 12.5, the old real seconds per game hour; clamp to the item's new duration), through the PU-11 migration table.
- **Tests (`StatusTimingSelfTests`):**
  - Regeneration adds ≥ 1.5 HP/s over natural regen.
  - Poison does 2 ± 0.2 HP/s, Burning 4 ± 0.4 HP/s.
  - A 4-min buff has ≥ 3:50 left after 10 s of sleeping.
  - A regen accessory gives ≥ 0.5 HP/s.
  - A 0.1.7 fixture save loads with its buffs converted.
- **Decision 3:** natural regen stays Terraria's formula only. No resting bonus is reintroduced.

### A3 · EV-4: events end at their boundary; expiry is not a clear
- Add `WorldEventSpec.EndsAt` (`Dawn`, `Dusk` or `null`) and `ClearsOnTimeout`:
  - true for weather, Aurora, Festival and Windy;
  - false for the moons, the Rust Moon and the Dark Sun.
- `Advance` (`WorldEvents.cs` ≈255-268) ends the event when its boundary passes, using BO-6's `BoundaryPassed` helper from 0.1.7.
- `completed` comes only from the cleared path: `RecordSeasonKill` reaching the final wave, or the quota met.
- **Tests (in `ClockSelfTests`):**
  - A Hollow Moon started at 23:00 ends at 06:00 with no cleared flag and 0 completions.
  - A Rust Moon at 02:00 ends at 06:00.
  - A Dark Sun at 15:00 ends at 18:00.
  - `WorldLeisureSelfTests` moon checks still pass. Update their expected timings.

## Stream B: Movement

### B1 · PU-1: Terraria movement feel (D-40, D-41)
- Give the player its own `PawnMovementSettings` (`GameSimulation.cs` ≈55). Colonists and creatures keep the defaults.
- **Targets** (Rimtera's own numbers, within ±10%):

  | Quantity | Target |
  |---|---|
  | Top run | ≈11 tiles/s |
  | Ground acceleration | ≈18 tiles/s² |
  | Friction | ≈45 tiles/s² |
  | Gravity | ≈90 tiles/s² |
  | Terminal speed | ≈37 tiles/s |
  | Jump impulse | ≈18.8 tiles/s, held up to ≈0.25 s (apex ≈6.5 tiles; a tap gives 1.5-2.5) |

- Rescale the hard-coded mobility values in `GameSimulation.Player.cs` and `GameSimulation.Gear.cs`:
  - stroke −4.6, double jump −8.6, wall jump, rocket −6.5;
  - glide caps 2.2/1.6, hook release −7.5;
  - mount cap 9 (moved to PU-6), `LiquidExitBoost`.
  Scale each by its Terraria counterpart ratio.
- The colonist `MoveSpeedMultiplier` no longer applies to the player character (D-41).
- **Camera follow:** check that D's snap camera (PE-16) keeps the player centred at 11 tiles/s.
- **Tests (`PlayerFeelSelfTests`)** on a flat 96×56 arena:
  - top speed 10-12 tiles/s;
  - 90% of top speed reached in 0.5-0.7 s;
  - stop in 0.2-0.3 s;
  - held apex 6-7 tiles, tap apex 1.5-2.5;
  - terminal speed 35-40 tiles/s.
  - Update the `ParityMovementTests` `A*` checks to the new numbers. They keep their meaning.

### B2 · PU-2: fall damage curve
- Damage = (tiles fallen − 25) × 10, measured from the highest point. Wings negate it.
- Mounts multiply it by a per-mount factor, set in PU-6 (0 for flyers).
- Featherfall, liquid and a latched hook still negate it (`GameSimulation.Player.cs` ≈389-409).
- **Test:** 25 tiles → 0, 26 → 10, 30 → 50, 35 → 100.

### B3 · PU-3: breath and drowning
- Base air ≈23 s; drowning ≈17 HP/s; refill ≈1 s.
- The rebreather tag gives ×6. Add a reed-type ×2 tag to an existing item, or a new appended `ItemId` (sprite placeholder).
- Files: `GameSimulation.Player.cs` ≈76-77, 411-440; item tags.
- **Test:** a submerged head reaches 0 breath at 23 ± 1 s; 100 HP lasts 5-7 s more; breath is full within 1.5 s after surfacing.

### B4 · PU-6: per-mount physics
- Add a fall factor, jump height, top speed and fall cap to each mount spec (`GameSimulation.Travel.cs`). Remove the global negate and the 9 tiles/s clamp (`Player.cs` ≈252-253, 384-386).
- **Tests:**
  - Each mount's apex and top speed match its spec ±10%.
  - The hover bike takes 0 fall damage.
  - A ground mount at 30 tiles takes its factor × 50.

### B5 · PU-7: swimming needs a flipper item (D-44)
- Without a `swim` tag: in deep water, jump is a hop with a short cooldown; climbing is capped at ≤ 2 tiles/s.
- With the tag: today's stroke applies.
- Add the `swim` tag to a flipper-analog item: reuse an existing diving item if there is one, otherwise append `ItemId` + recipe at the Tinker Bench + a fishing crate source.
- Files: `GameSimulation.Player.cs` ≈293-299.
- **Tests:** a self-test without and with the gear. `--craft-audit` shows the item obtainable before the first boss.

## Stream C: Respawn, Creative, saves

### C1 · PU-5 and PC-10: Terraria respawn timer (D-7)
- `GetRespawnDelay`: 10 s base; ×1.5 on Brutal and Annihilation; Explorer 3 s. No single-player boss extension; 0.1.7's BO-16 stays as the guard.
- `UpdateWhileDead` sets `_respawnSeconds = GetRespawnDelay(...)`, so the internal timer and the UI countdown agree (`GameSimulation.Player.cs` ≈95-96, 1021-1032).
- **Tests:** Classic 10 s, Brutal 15 s, Explorer 3 s, and 10 s with a living boss. `RespawnSecondsRemaining` equals the UI countdown.

### C2 · IC-6: bed spawn point
- Interacting with a bed in a valid room sets the spawn point. Reuse the housing room check (`GameSimulation.Town.cs` or `Leisure.cs`).
- Save it per character per world: the `PlayerPositions` map from PU-9 gains a `SpawnPoint`.
- Respawn at the bed, or at the landing site if the bed is gone, with a message ("Your bed is missing or obstructed").
- **Test:** set a bed, die → the player stands beside it. Break the bed, die → the landing site.

### C3 · PU-10: Creative isolation (D-46)
- `WorldProfile.CreatedByCreative`, set at creation. The menu greys out non-Creative worlds for a Creative character and the reverse, with an explanation line (`PreferencesAndProfiles.cs`, `MenuSystem.cs` ≈1066-1080).
- `--continue` refuses a mismatched pair.
- This also closes G13 (the Journey world lock).
- **Test:** a Creative character sees Classic worlds disabled, and the reverse.

### C4 · PC-13 and X-CR-10: research on the character; Journey powers
- Store research counts in `CharacterFileData`, as an appended dictionary. On load, migrate any `creative.research.*` world flags into the character (`Creative.cs` ≈13-60, `CharacterFile.cs`).
- Add the missing Journey powers: weather control (rain and wind sliders through the global weather model; EV-9 later reads it) and enemy-spread freeze (blight and crystal spread off).
- **Tests:**
  - Research an item in world A, create world B with the same character: still researched.
  - The spread freeze stops `WorldTicker` conversion.

### C5 · PC-14: Creative kit polish
- Replace the 3 recall capsules with a reusable recall device: an existing item if present, otherwise append `ItemId`, using the recall capsule effect with no consume.
- Pre-summon the drone uplink's drone on first entry (`StarterKits.cs` ≈22-27, `GrowthItems.cs` ≈313-327).
- **Test (`StarterKitSelfTests`):** a Creative first entry holds a reusable recall item and has one drone active.

### C6 · PU-12: autosave cadence and threading (D-42)
- Capture the snapshot on the game thread, then serialise and write on a worker, as `SaveWithScreen` does (`GraphicalSandbox.cs` ≈983-988, 1382-1404).
- Skip a tick if a save is still running. Writes keep PU-11's backup rotation.
- Add the preference `AutosaveMinutes` ∈ {2, 5, 10, 15, off}, default 10, saved in `GamePreferences`.
- **Tests:** the preference round-trips. Measure with `--engine-bench --only Large`: the UI-thread time of an autosave is ≤ 17 ms.

- Use 3 × 1024-frame buffers (≈70 ms), or WASAPI shared mode if the current backend allows it.

## Risks
- **EV-17 ripple.** Every per-hour quantity needs a classification. Mitigation: the A1 grep list goes into the PR description as a table of each site and its decision. Plus the colonist invariance test.
- **PU-1 breaks hidden assumptions.** Jump-tuned worldgen, enemy chase speeds and knockback all assumed the old movement. Mitigation: later steps (EN-2, WT-10/11, BO-1) re-measure. Note any visibly broken structure as a 0.1.11 input.
- **Buff migration.** Old saves hold hour-based durations. Mitigation: the migration hook, plus the fixture check.
