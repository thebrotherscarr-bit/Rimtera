# 0.1.9: Combat core

**Master plan:** [ROADMAP_0.2.0.md](../ROADMAP_0.2.0.md) · **Depends on:** 0.1.7 (IC-4 HP-only player, IC-7 minion lifecycle), 0.1.8 (IC-1 real-time status units, PU-1 speeds) · **Size:** L (≈35 agent-days; 4 streams)

**IDs (20):** EN-4, IC-10, EN-5, EN-18, IC-3, EN-17, EN-12, IC-18, IC-21, IC-15, IC-2, BO-11, EN-10, EN-11, IC-19, IC-17, PE-4, PE-5, PE-7, PE-14. **Rollup:** D-18 (no hardmode rescale), D-21, D-22, D-59, D-61.

## Goals
- **Defense matters.** Creatures get tiered defense, knockback resistance and explicit immunities, and take lava damage. The double hardmode rescale goes (D-18). Banner and despawn numbers come from the IL.
- **Danger flows both ways.** Creatures inflict Terraria-style debuffs on the player. Weapons inflict more afflictions on creatures.
- **Hits are fair.** Contact uses box overlap. Damage variance is ±15%. Immune players are not hit by projectiles. Gear stays consistent.
- **Sustain exists.**
  - A 4-tier healing and charge potion ladder, plus a restoration potion and potion sickness.
  - Boss potions and hearts.
  - Heart and charge pickups (D-61).
  - Drop chances and conditions, and event creatures drop items (D-22).
  - Stacks of 9999.
- **Feedback.**
  - Every action emits a simulation sound event.
  - Despawns are silent.
  - Block dust and death gore.
  - Crit, DoT and charge combat text.
  - A truthful item tooltip.

## Stream A: Creature stats

1. **Measure first.** Extend `--balance` to print a defense column and time-to-kill per tier. Save the "before" numbers.
2. **Data.** Add a defense tier table, applied in the generator and the hand-authored rosters:

   | Tier | Defense |
   |---|---|
   | Pre-boss | 0-6 (gel 2, walker 6) |
   | Mid pre-Fracture | 8-14 |
   | Post-Fracture | 16-30 |
   | Late | 30-50 |
   | Bosses | from their analog band |

   Add an optional `KnockbackResist` override (it falls back to today's formula).
3. **D-18.** Remove the post-Fracture ×1.7 life / ×1.3 damage rescale for creatures **authored** for the Fracture. Terraria never rescales; only the pools swap. Pre-Fracture kinds that still spawn keep their stats (`GameSimulation.Hardmode.cs` ≈52-59).
4. **Retune `GearCurve`.** Only if the time-to-kill bands break. Prefer data changes to formula changes.
- **Tests (`CreatureDefenseSelfTests`):**
  - Every hostile non-boss kind with > 150 HP has `Defense > 0`.
  - A 10-damage hit on defense 20 does 1 before penetration and more with it (Normal world: 50% defense).
  - An armor-penetration accessory measurably raises damage on armored creatures.
  - The `StatParitySelfTests` and `GearCurveSelfTests` bands pass.
  - No post-Fracture-authored kind is scaled twice.

## Stream C: Healing, loot, pickups

### C1 · IC-2: the healing and charge potion ladder
- **Append `ItemId`s:**

  - a restoration-type potion: 90 HP, 45 s sickness;

- **Gates** (recipes): Chem Lab → the Bio Lab tier (post first boss) → post-Fracture Alloy-tier lab materials → the endgame (Void Forge materials).
- Add them to the Medic and Quartermaster shops by flag.
- **Potion sickness:** 60 s. Add an accessory tag `potion-sickness:0.75` on an existing alchemist-style accessory (if there is none, IC-16 adds one in 0.1.14).
- **Food:** cooked food heals a small amount on use (deliberate use, so decision 3 holds) and gives Well Fed tiers (NC-2 follow-up).
- QuickHeal picks the **smallest tier that covers the missing HP**, otherwise the largest.
- **Sprites:** recolours of the Med stim. Real art is queued.
- **Tests (`HealingLadderSelfTests`):**
  - Each tier exists and is craftable only after its gate (`--craft-audit`).
  - QuickHeal picks correctly at 30, 90 and 180 missing HP.
  - Sickness 60 s; 45 s with the accessory.
  - The balance check: at each tier band, the median creature hit is ≤ 50% of that tier's potion.

### C2 · BO-11: boss potions and hearts (D-61)
- Every boss drops 5-15 healing items scaled by tier:
  - Gate/Easy → tier 1;
  - Hard → tier 2;
  - Event/Final → tiers 3-4;
- plus 3-5 heart pickups (EN-10's item), outside the salvage cache, on every difficulty (`GameSimulation.cs` ≈1693-1706 or `PlayerSystems.cs` ≈398-416).
- **Test:** every boss kill leaves ≥ 5 healing items of the matching tier on all three difficulties. Use `BossSelfTests`' spawn harness.

### C3 · EN-10: heart and charge pickups (D-61)
- On a kill, a chance to drop a heart (heals 20) or a charge orb (+100 charge). The chance is weighted toward a hurt player or low charge, Terraria's `NPCLoot_DropHeals` shape.
- Picking one up applies the effect immediately and never enters the inventory (`GroundItems.cs`, `ItemSystem.cs` flags `IsInstantPickup`).
- **Tests:** over 200 kills at half health, 15-60 hearts drop; a pickup heals and never stacks.

### C4 · EN-11: drop rules (D-22)

- Give the 48 event units item drops: their event materials, plus a rare signature item each where the arsenal has one.

- **Tests (`DropRuleSelfTests`):** a 1/500 rule hits within 0.5-3× the expected rate in 50,000 rolls; conditions refuse off-condition; `--craft-audit` still reports every item obtainable.

### C5 · IC-19: stack sizes

## Stream D: Feedback

### D1 · PE-4: simulation sound events (D-59)
- Add a `SoundEvents` queue in `GameSimulation`, holding (kind, variant, position). The simulation fills it; `AudioDirector` drains it with `Near` and `Pan`.
- **Emit:**
  - `break` with a material variant (stone, soil, plant, glass, metal) on block destroy;
  - `dig` on every pick, drill, axe or hammer hit, channelled drills included;
  - `place` for walls;
  - Tech and Command use sounds;
  - `craft` on success, and stop `pickup` firing for crafts;
  - `coin`, drink and eat;
  - `door` and chest open and close;
  - `splash` on entering or leaving liquid;
  - `explosion`, player death, double jump.
  - **No** sound for a plain jump or landing (D-59).
- Add synth fallbacks in `AudioEngine` for eat, drink, chest, grab and the death sting.
- **Test (`SoundEventSelfTests`):** drive a headless simulation through mine-to-break, wall place, craft, drink, door, splash and Tech and summon uses. One event of each kind; none on a plain jump.

### D2 · PE-5: despawns are not kills
- The simulation records `RecentDeaths` with (position, reason Died/Despawned/Caught). `ActorAnimator` bursts and `AudioDirector` plays `death` only for `Died`. Bug-net catches play a soft catch sound.
- **Tests:** despawning 20 off-screen enemies produces no death events. Play-test: standing still at night, no stray death sounds.

### D3 · PE-7: block dust and death gore
- A material-coloured dust emitter on every mining hit (2-4 particles) and on break (8-12). The colour is the tile sprite's average, cached per tile like `FootColor`.
- Per-creature death chunks: 3-5 fragments cut from the idle frame, with gravity and a fade. Driven by D1's events.
- **Tests:** a play-test shows distinct dust colours for dirt, stone and ore; the particle cap of 700 holds with a drill held for 10 s (F10 "effects" < 1 ms).

### D4 · PE-14: combat text parity
- Hit records: (target, amount, crit, kind direct/dot/heal/charge).
  - Crits are drawn larger and orange.
  - DoT is smaller and dimmer, with no hit sound.
  - Charge gains are blue.
  - Text stacks for rapid hits.
- **Tests:** a forced crit gives a crit record; a play-test shows orange crit numbers.

### D5 · IC-17: the item tooltip builder
- Add `Systems/ItemTooltip.cs`: `Build(ItemStack, context)` → lines. It covers:
  - class-correct damage ("42 tech damage", "drone damage");
  - ammo family, crit, speed words from use ticks, knockback words, charge cost;
  - calibration name and deltas;
  - defense, set name and bonus;
  - accessory tag effects in plain words;
  - rarity colour;
  - the sell value at an open shop.
- `GraphicalSandbox.cs` (≈3250) renders it.
- **Tests (`TooltipSelfTests`):** over all items (≈1,981), no ranged item claims "Energy cells" unless it uses cells, and every weapon has a damage line.

## Release acceptance (0.1.9)

**Self-tests:** `CreatureDefenseSelfTests`, `PlayerDebuffSelfTests`, `HealingLadderSelfTests`, `DropRuleSelfTests`, `SoundEventSelfTests` and `TooltipSelfTests` registered. `StatParity` and `GearCurve` pass. 0 FAIL.

**Audits:**
- `--balance`: defense column present; time-to-kill per tier within the existing bands.
- `--craft-audit`: 0/0/0, potion tiers at their gates.
- `--item-audit`: clean (new items have functions and placeholder sprites).
- `--entity-sprites`: 0 missing.
- 0.1.8 fixture saves load.

**Play-test checklist:**
1. Fight a pre-boss walker: armor matters. A copper-tier weapon hits for less on a defense-6 creature.
2. A spider poisons you; the buff bar shows a red debuff; an immunity accessory blocks it.
3. Craft and drink each healing tier as it unlocks. QuickHeal picks sensibly. Sickness is 60 s.
4. Kill creatures while hurt: hearts drop and heal on touch. Kill a boss: potions and hearts.
5. Mining has dust and sounds. Crafting chimes. Doors, chests, potions, coins and splashes all sound.
6. Creatures leaving at dawn make no death sounds or bursts.
7. Crit numbers are orange. The tooltip of a Tech weapon shows its damage.
8. An auto-swing glove makes a shortsword auto-swing.

## Risks
- **Time-to-kill shifts everywhere.** Mitigation: the balance numbers go into the PR before and after; keep the bands; PC-15 does the real curve in 0.2.0.
- **IC-3 Confused touches input.** Mitigation: invert in the simulation's movement input, never in the UI input layer. Phase 2 owns that layer.
- **Sound spam.** Mitigation: per-kind rate limits in `AudioDirector`.
