## Stream A: Balance

### A1 · `--boss-balance` report (the tool first)
`Systems/BossBalance.cs` simulates each boss against a reference player kitted for that boss's progression tier. The kit comes from the craft audit's stage at which the boss becomes reachable: the best weapon, armour and accessories obtainable by then, plus the matching potion tier. It reports for each boss:
- hits to kill and seconds to kill, using the measured DPS of the tier weapon set;
- incoming damage per hit and per second against tier armour;
- survival time without healing, and with the tier potion on cooldown.

It runs on Classic, Brutal and Annihilation.

### A2 · PC-15: re-curve boss life against player damage
- **Target curve.** Time to kill rises roughly linearly with progression index. The final boss's TTK is at least 2× the first boss's; Terraria's ratio is about 2.6.
- **Changes**, in order of preference:
  1. Raise late-boss life: Fission Tyrant ≈2,500, trio 4-6k, Astro-God ≈20-25k. These numbers predate the 500-life player and the 0.1.9 defense; re-derive them from A1.
  2. Lower the tier-6 and tier-7 weapon damage bases.
  3. Make `BossDamageCap` scale per tier instead of a flat 100, or remove it if the curve holds without it.
- **D-6 difficulty scaling.**
  - Brutal: ≈×2 creature life and ×2 damage. Annihilation: ≈×3.
  - Hardmode multipliers stay off for Fracture-authored creatures (D-18).
  - Defense effectiveness stays 50/75/100% (G3, already a match).
- **Tests (`BalanceSelfTests`):**
  - The `--boss-balance` hits-to-kill rise with progression: Spearman correlation ≥ 0.8 across the 11 progression bosses.
  - Astro-God hits ≥ 2× Overseer hits.
  - Brutal life is 1.9-2.1× Classic; Annihilation is 2.8-3.2×.

### A3 · BO-23: boss balance pass
- Tune every boss (33) and mini-boss (9):
  - time to kill 60-180 s with tier gear;
  - incoming damage survivable with tier armour and potions: survival without healing ≥ 15 s, and indefinite with potions on cooldown and dodging (assume 50% of shots avoided).
- Record the final table in `docs/CREATURE_COVERAGE.md`.
- **Test:** the `--boss-balance` band check passes for every boss on Classic. Report Brutal and Annihilation, but do not gate on them.

## Stream B: CI gates

### B1 · PE-20: asset and performance gates
- **`--asset-check`** (`Systems/AssetCheck.cs`) asserts two things:
  - Every sprite the game can request exists in `git ls-files`: items, tiles, flora, actors and their frames, minion and projectile sprites, worn-armour layers and backgrounds.
  - No generated frame is over its size budget: 512 px on the long side, from PE-2.
- **Prove matrix.** Add a `prover: assets` entry running `--asset-check`.
- **Bench job.** Add `--engine-bench --only Small` with soft budgets, written to the step summary and failing only on a 2× regression:
  - Small generation ≤ 8 s;
  - tick ≤ 5 ms;
  - flood worst ≤ 50 ms;
  - title memory ≤ 450 MB.
- **Tests:**
  - A local run deleting one frame PNG from the index makes `--asset-check` fail.
  - The CI step summary shows the bench numbers.

## Stream C: Polish

### C1 · PE-11: coloured lighting on High
- On High quality, present the light as a multiply layer: a per-channel tint of the scene, not darkness plus a capped glow tint. Standard stays as it is for speed (`LightMap.cs` `Present`, `Draw`). It uses PE-10's coloured sky light.

### D3 · Full Terraria-parity play-through (sign-off)
Two full runs on Release builds: Classic on Medium, and Brutal on Small. Use a new character each time and play without debug keys. Check each of these:
1. **Opening.** The kit, a tree, the Fabricator, the guidance tips. The player never acts alone and has no needs bars. The day lasts 24 minutes.
2. **Early game.**
   - Each drill opens new ore.
   - Shooters shoot and fighters climb.

   - The Rust Moon has no boss.
   - A raid marches in at dawn.
   - The Mechanic is freed after the raid, sells the Tinker Bench and reforges.

   - The Fission Tyrant wall works.
4. **Hardmode.**
   - Wide scars.
   - Altars seed ore by tier and depth.
   - The trio as a worm, arms and twins.

5. **Endgame.**
   - The obelisks need 100 kills each.
   - The Astro-God in phases.
   - Life reaches 500.
6. **Colony.** Crew live at home, guard, garden, haul and cook. They never touch the player's inventory and never heal the player.
7. **Presentation.**
   - Every action has a sound.
   - Event skies.
   - Per-tier boss music.
   - Visible armour.
   - Memory stays within budget on Large.
8. **Saves.** Autosave every 10 minutes. Closing the window saves. A 0.1.7 save loads in 0.2.0.

## Risks

- **Balance finds upstream bugs.** Fix them in place, with the owning area's tests, and keep the 3-day buffer.
- **Numbers keep moving.** Freeze the balance tables once A3 passes; D documents the frozen values.
- **A soft gate becomes noise.** Bench gates fail only on a 2× regression; everything else is reported.
