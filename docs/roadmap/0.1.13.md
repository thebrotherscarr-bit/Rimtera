## Goals

- **The colony serves the Terraria loop (decision 7, D-49).** Crew members:
  - live in their own claimed room and stay there in danger;
  - guard the town;
  - work as gardener, hauler, cook and (optionally) miner, using chests and stations only;
  - get better with practice;
  - are replaced at a capped rate.
- **They never touch the player's inventory, never heal or feed the player, and raids come to the colony, not the player's tunnel (D-54).**
- **Town NPCs fight back.** The housing menu lists residents and can assign and evict.
- **Terraria economics:**
  - happiness factors and a report;
  - pylons sold by happy residents (D-50);
  - reforging from the tinkerer, the Mechanic (D-5), who also sells the Tinker Bench;
  - calibration value and rarity;
  - a Medic that clears debuffs;
  - a shop grid with buyback and Ctrl-click sell.
- **Progression-gated residents (D-9, D-51, D-53):**
  - the Medic needs > 100 life and the Quartermaster;
  - the Armorer needs a firearm;
  - survivors are freed after their bosses;
  - the Trade Consul becomes a permanent resident.
- **New roles (D-52):** a Stylist with the appearance editor, a tax-collector analog and a fungal vendor.
- **Distinct NPC sprites and deeper dialogue.**

## Stream A: Crew life

### A1 · NC-3: crew live at home and claim their room
- `ColonistAgent.HomeCell` is set on arrival (`Town.cs` ≈84-89). `CheckHousing` and `FindFreeHouse` treat crew homes as claimed.
- Remove "Following" and the `HomePosition = player` drift (`GameSimulation.cs` ≈1366-1413).
- Crew wander within ≈12 tiles of home and shelter at night and during events like residents. In danger they stay home.
- Creatures may still target crew (that is the guard's job, NC-5).
- Save `HomeCell` in `ColonistSnapshot`, appended. On migration, old crew claim the nearest free house or stay homeless.
- **Tests (`CrewSelfTests`):**
  - A crew room reports "already claimed".
  - With the player 150 tiles away for 2 game hours, every crew member is within 12 tiles of home.
  - Homes survive save and load.

### A2 · NC-18: crew replacement
- Cap **living** crew at 5. After a death, wait 24 game hours before a newcomer arrives, if a house is free (`Town.cs` ≈81-90, `Storyteller.cs` ≈436).
- **Test:** 5 joins and 1 death → a sixth member arrives 24 h later.

### A3 · NC-21: dead code
- Remove the unregistered `WandererJoinsIncident` (`Storyteller.cs` ≈434-451). Keep `MaximumCrew` elsewhere.
- C deletes `NpcHappiness.cs` in C1.
- **Test:** the build passes with no references.

### A4 · NC-5: crew defend the town
- A crew member with a weapon kind (from the Shooting or Melee skill) attacks hostiles within 10 tiles of home. Use the 0.1.9 projectile and hit API: player-side projectiles that do not hurt the player.
- Damage scales with skill and with the bosses defeated (the tier table).
- Below 30% HP, crew flee indoors.
- Only combat-capable crew count toward raid threat (`Storyteller.cs` ≈135-140).
- **Tests:**
  - A slime 6 tiles from a housed guard dies without the player acting.
  - Raid points don't rise for crew with no combat skill.

### A5 · NC-19: raids hit the colony (D-54)
- If the player is within ≈150 tiles of the colony centre (the centroid of housed residents), the raid spawns at the colony edge and walks in. Reuse EV-5's `InvasionFront` mechanics.
- Otherwise the raid is held until the player returns.
- Threat counts combat crew only (`GameSimulation.cs` ≈2321-2350, `Storyteller.cs` ≈338-354).
- **Test:** a raid forced while the player is 300 tiles down a cave does not spawn. It fires on return, ≥ 40 tiles from the nearest house, moving toward it.

## Stream B: Crew jobs and orders

### B1 · NC-4: jobs that feed the Terraria loop (D-49)
- A small job system in `GameSimulation.CrewJobs.cs`, with jobs chosen per crew member by skill.
  - **Gardener:** harvests planters and hydroponics into the nearest town chest, and replants.
  - **Hauler:** carries ground items within the town radius into chests.
  - **Cook:** turns raw food from a chest into Well Fed food at a cooking station, using the normal recipes, into a chest.
  - **Guard:** A4's behaviour as a job stance.
  - **Miner (optional, last):** digs a player-marked area. Add a "mark for mining" designation tool item, which puts the result into a chest. Cut it if the step runs long (master §8.4).
- Skills set the speed.
- Jobs use **only** town chests and stations. They never read or write `Player.Inventory` and never apply anything to the player.
- **Tests (one per job):**
  - A gardener with ripe planters raises a chest's produce each game day.
  - A hauler empties 20 ground items into a chest within 1 game hour.
  - A cook turns 5 raw food into cooked food.
  - The player's inventory hash is unchanged across all job tests.

### B2 · NC-17: skills grow
- XP from tending, foraging and jobs. Passions multiply it ×1.5 and ×2.
- `SkillLevels` become mutable and saved (append-only field). The "passions learn faster" text becomes true.
- **Test:** a medic who tends 30 times gains a Medical level, and a burning passion gains faster than none.

### B3 · NC-25: colony orders UI
- A Work page in the colonist sheet with per-crew job toggles and a "Stay home / Guard" stance.
- **Test:** turning a crew member's Hauler job off stops hauling within 1 game hour.

## Stream C: Town economy and reforge

### C1 · NC-10: happiness parity
- **Multiplicative factors:**
  - biome love/like/dislike/hate: ×0.88/0.94/1.06/1.12, and the same scale for neighbours;
  - solitude: ×0.95 with ≤ 2 neighbours;
  - crowding: ×1.05 per neighbour above 3;
  - homeless or more than 120 tiles from home: clamp to 1.5;
  - round to 0.01.
- Neighbours are measured from homes and exclude gels, pets, the merchant and bound survivors.
- The sell price no longer divides by the multiplier.
- Add a happiness report with one line per factor (`GraphicalSandbox.cs` ≈1119).
- Delete `Systems/NpcHappiness.cs`.
- **Tests (`HappinessSelfTests`):**
  - love biome alone → 0.84;
  - 5 neighbours → ×1.1025;
  - homeless → 1.50;
  - a gel next door changes nothing;
  - the sell price equals value / 5.

### C2 · NC-11: pylons tied to happy towns (D-50)
- A resident with price ≤ 0.90 whose home is in a pylon biome sells that pylon. Remove the anvil recipe.
- Pylon activity counts only real residents (no gels and no merchant).
- Teleporting is refused while a boss lives or a hostile is within 10 tiles.
- **Tests:**
  - A happy Field Zoologist in the canopy offers the Canopy pylon, and loses the offer when crowded.
  - A pylon with 1 resident and 2 gels is dormant.
  - Teleporting with an adjacent slime fails with a message.

### C3 · NC-12 and IC-13: reforging (D-5)
- The Mechanic gets "Recalibrate held item". Cost = value × ⅓ × the happiness multiplier.
- The roll uses the crafting roller (`GameSimulation.cs` ≈789) inside the item's group. Tools become calibratable in the melee group, so speed matters for mining. Thrown consumables stop calibrating, so stacks always merge (I8).
- Loot, boss drops and chest weapons roll a calibration, 25% of them with none.
- **Tests (`ReforgeSelfTests`):**
  - 50 recalibrations of a sword charge credits each time, give ≥ 5 distinct results and never leave the melee group.
  - It refuses with too few credits.
  - 1,000 boss-weapon drops are ≈25% uncalibrated.
  - A calibrated pick with a speed calibration mines faster.

### C4 · IC-14: calibration value and rarity
- Value × valueMult², and rarity ±1/±2 from valueMult. The tooltip name colour shows rarity (0.1.9 `ItemTooltip`; `GameSimulation.Town.cs` ≈389, `ContentItems.RarityFor`).
- **Test:** a Masterwork weapon sells for more than 2× a plain one, and its name colour moves up a tier.

### C5 · NC-13: Medic parity
- The heal also clears harmful statuses. Cost = (missing HP + 2 per debuff) × (1 + 0.5 per boss tier) × the multiplier (`Town.cs` ≈412-423).
- It stays a paid, deliberate action, so decision 3 holds.
- **Test:** a poisoned player at 50/100 pays more after a boss kill than before, and leaves without Poison.

### C6 · NC-15: gels and pets don't trade
- Hide "Shop" and "Sell held item" when the shop is empty or `IsGel`. `Sell` returns 0 for gels.
- **Test:** `Sell(gel, slot) == 0`, and the talk menu has no shop entries.

### C7 · PU-21 and X-CP-5: shop grid with buyback; Ctrl-click sells
- A shop panel in `InventoryUiState`, a slot grid with prices, beside the player grid.
  - Click buys; holding right-click buys repeatedly (`StackSplitRepeater`).
  - Shift-click or **Ctrl-click** on any grid item sells it while a shop is open. This is Terraria's `GetSellOrTrash`; favourites are exempt.
  - A buyback row holds the last sold stacks.
- **Tests:**
  - Selling slot 7 adds credits and puts the stack in buyback.
  - Buying it back costs the sell price.
  - Ctrl-click sells with a shop open and trashes without one.

### C8 · X-CR-5: the Dye Vat is sold only
- Remove the Dye Vat recipe. The Pigment Trader sells it. Update `CRAFTING_PROGRESSION.md`.
- **Test:** `--craft-audit` shows the vat sourced from the shop only.

- **Tests:**

  - A bow alone does not unlock the Armorer.

## Risks
- **Colony behaviour is open-ended.**
  - Mitigation: jobs touch only chests and stations, and the inventory-hash invariant is tested.
  - If the step runs long, the miner job and the third role move after 0.2.0.
- **Survivor re-gating changes worldgen.**
  - Mitigation: old worlds keep their existing free survivors (grandfathered); only new worlds gate.
- **`Town.cs` is shared by C and D.**
  - Mitigation: function-level ownership as above; C lands its happiness block before D's move-ins.
