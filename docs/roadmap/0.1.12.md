## Goals
- **Event rosters spawn only where they belong.** Weather, moons and raids spawn on the surface; obelisk rosters spawn near their obelisk.
- **Natural triggers follow Terraria's shape:**
  - no Rust Moon on a new moon;
  - Swarm Rain on dry mornings, falling from the sky;
  - invasions roll at dawn, with a front that marches in;
  - an Aurora Night after a first clear, with no spawn suppression (D-26);
  - meteor nights after the second-tier boss;
  - the Festival needs a host;
  - a wave moon cancels a Rust Moon.
- **Event finales** (D-24): none before the Fracture; after it, mini-boss finales.
- **The Martian analog** waits for the Golem analog and starts from an escaping scout.
- **The Defense Protocol** becomes a 3-tier defend-the-core event, with pauses, a fail state, medals and a shop.
- **Weather:**
  - one global climate, rendered per biome;
  - lightning independent of frame rate, visual only (D-25);
  - acid rain does no damage (D-25);
  - wind pushes the player and applies once to shots;
  - Windy Day as a weather state.
- **Moons** have 15 and 20 waves (D-27), and loot scales with the wave.
- **Small systems:** the grave field becomes a real mini-biome; sundial and moondial analogs; info gear and readouts.
- **Presentation:**
  - event skies and coloured sky light;
  - music crossfade with event, night, boss-tier and biome themes, plus a recorded-track loader (D-55);
  - ambient beds;
  - weather polish.

## Stream A: Event framework and triggers

### A1 · EV-3: event spawns depend on location
- Add `WorldEventSpec.SpawnScope` with three cases:
  - `Surface`: weather, the Rust Moon, the Dark Sun, raids, Swarm Rain, Windy Day.
  - `Anywhere`: the meteor at its crater, the Lumen Tempest underground.
  - `NearAnchor(x, radius)`: obelisks, the raid front.
- `PickSpawn(cell)` lets only matching rosters take the roll.
- Weather rosters also need their biome, using `BiomeScanner` from 0.1.11:
  - blizzard → Frozen;
  - dust → Junk;
  - acid → coast, blight or the Fracture.
- **Tests (`EventScopeSelfTests`):**
  - Force rain with the player in the dungeon, and again 40 tiles deep. 200 spawns include none from a weather roster.
  - An obelisk roster creature is never more than R tiles from its obelisk.

### A2 · EV-5: natural triggers in Terraria's shape (D-24, D-26)
- **Rust Moon:** never in phase 4 (the dark moon).
- **Swarm Rain:** only on dry mornings (rain < 0.2, before 09:00). Creatures spawn high and fall.
- **Scavenger and pirate rolls:**
  - They move to dawn.
  - The army marches from a world edge toward the colony: an `InvasionFront` x that moves each hour.
  - Announce it at a quarter and at half distance.
  - Expose the front for NC-19 (0.1.13).
- **Aurora Night:**
  - Queued for the night after any first boss clear or first event clear, through a hook on `boss.*.down` / `event.*.cleared`.
  - A rare chance after the Astro-God, with a 5-10 night cooldown.
  - It cannot start with a Rust Moon, a moon, an invasion or a living boss.
  - Remove its `NoHostileSpawns` (D-26).
- **Meteor nights:** a small nightly chance once the second-tier boss (the Alpha Skitter or Mining Drone tier, per area 01) is down.
- **Festival:** needs the Party Planner (the host NPC). Remove `NoHostileSpawns` (D-26).
- **Wave moons:** starting one cancels a running Rust Moon.
- **Finales (D-24):** pre-Fracture events have no finale (already true after 0.1.7). Post-Fracture finales are mini-bosses with the 0.1.10 patterns.
- **Tests:**
  - A 1,000-night roll table has no Rust Moon on phase 4 and no Swarm Rain while raining.
  - The night after the first Overseer kill is an Aurora Night.
  - A raid's front moves toward the spawn column for at least 2 game hours before creatures spawn.
  - The Festival does not suppress hostiles.

### A3 · EV-6: the Martian-analog gate and its scout trigger
- Gate Precursor Probes on the Vault Colossus (the Golem analog). Remove the plain dusk roll (`WorldEvents.cs` ≈448-453).
- Add a "Precursor scout" creature:
  - It spawns in the outer thirds of the surface or sky after the gate.
  - If it sees the player and then leaves the screen alive, the invasion starts.
  - Optional starter item: a "probe beacon".
- **Tests:**
  - With only `ore.voidsteel` set, 200 dusks start no event.
  - With the gate set, an escaping scout starts the event, and one killed in time does not.

### A4 · EV-13: moon scoring shape (D-27)
- The Hollow Moon has 15 waves and the Rime Moon has 20.
- The per-kill drop chance scales with the wave (≥ 2× by wave 8 compared with wave 1).
- The main event bar shows "wave n · score x/y". The leisure line is removed.
- Moon items are gated on the Mutated Flora flag (the Plantera analog) rather than the Fracture (`GameSimulation.Seasons.cs`, `GraphicalSandbox.cs` ≈2926, `GraphicalSandbox.Leisure.cs` ≈131).
- **Tests:** the wave-8 drop share is ≥ 2× the wave-1 share. The bar data holds the score.

## Stream B: Defense Protocol

### B1 · EV-7: defend-the-core (builds on PC-2 from 0.1.7)
- **The pylon.** Using the signal core places a "Signal pylon" actor at the Station AI. It has HP and cannot be healed.
- **Pathing.** Event creatures path to the pylon with EN-2's pathing and hit it.
- **Failure.** Losing the pylon fails the event with a message. The core is kept.
- **Pauses.** Waves have a pause between them, which the player can skip at the Station AI.
- **Tiers**, each with its own spec, roster, wave count and finale:

  | Tier | Gate | Finale |
  |---|---|---|
  | T1 | Pre-Fracture | None, per D-24 |
  | T2 | After any hard-trio boss | Ogre-class mini-boss |
  | T3 | After the Vault Colossus | Protocol Dragon |

- **Medals.** Medal tokens drop per wave and feed a Station AI shop (`TownNpcs.cs`).
- **Building.** Building near the pylon is blocked while the event runs (Terraria's `ShouldBlockBuilding` shape).
- **Tests (`DefenseProtocolSelfTests`):**
  - Destroying the pylon ends the event with `completed: false` and no cleared flag.
  - Clearing every wave of T1 sets `event.defense_protocol.t1.cleared`.
  - T2 refuses before its gate.
  - Medals drop.

## Stream C: Weather and wind

### C1 · EV-9: weather from the world's climate (D-25)
- **One global model.** Roll one global wind, cloud and rain model for the world. Each biome renders and applies it locally:
  - rain becomes snow in the Frozen zone;
  - rain becomes acid on the coast, in blight and in the Fracture;
  - a dust storm only in Junk, and only when |wind| ≥ the threshold;
  - a blizzard is snow plus strong wind.
- **Wind and storms.**
  - Couple the wind to the rain, and use wind spells of 2-8 h instead of four fixed rolls.
  - Storms come from cloud and wind thresholds.
- **Acid rain does no damage (D-25).**
- **Journey control.** The weather power (PC-13/X-CR-10, 0.1.8) drives the global model.
- **Saves.** `WeatherState` gains append-only fields.
- **Tests (`WeatherClimateSelfTests`):**
  - Walk from Frozen to Grassland during snow: the snow stops while the rain value stays.
  - A dust storm never starts below the wind threshold.
  - The storm-hour share is within ±2% between runs at 60 Hz and at 4 Hz.

### C2 · EV-10: lightning independent of the frame rate (D-25)
- Roll strikes per game second, a Poisson rate from rain × wind, in `UpdateWorldRealtime`.
- **Visual only:** no damage strikes. Sky flashes with a thunder delay.
- Rare flashes over a grave field (EV-14).
- **Test:** a forced storm gives the same strike count (±20%) over 60 s at 60 Hz and at 4 Hz.

### C3 · EV-11: wind pushes the player and applies once to shots
- When |wind| ≥ the windy threshold, the player is outdoors on the surface, and airborne or on a light surface, add a horizontal push and a status icon. A set of immune items cancels it.
- Remove the spawn-time `WindDrift` (`Combat.cs` ≈322), so `ApplyWind` alone moves shots. Add a per-weapon wind immunity for beams and lasers.
- **Tests:**
  - A jumping player drifts ≥ 1 tile downwind over 2 s on a windy day, and 0 underground.
  - A bullet's x-velocity gain from wind equals `ApplyWind`'s alone.

### C4 · EV-12: Windy Day as a weather state
- "Windy" is a derived state: |wind| ≥ the threshold, a clear sky, morning to midday. The event spec is weather-synced: no fixed 10 h and no forced 7.5.
- Its roster spawns only in windy surface areas. D supplies the windy theme.
- **Test:** with the wind forced to 0 during a Windy Day, the event ends and a kite drops.

## Stream D: Atmosphere, sky, music, ambience

### D1 · EV-8 and PE-10: event skies and coloured sky light
- `GameSimulation.EventAtmosphere` is derived from the active events. It carries a sky tint, the sun and moon colour, a light multiplier and a music theme:
  - the Rust Moon is red;
  - the Dark Sun has a black sun with a corona and 0.35× daylight;
  - the Aurora shows green ribbons;
  - the Festival shows balloons;
  - each obelisk tints the sky near it;
  - the grave field adds fog and dims the light;
  - storms get the storm theme.
- `SkyLightLevel` becomes `SkyLight` (RGB):
  - warm at dawn and dusk, cool at night;
  - event tints on top.
- `LightMap.Compute` takes the colour. `DrawSky` blends the event gradients. The sun gets an eclipse overlay. Biome backgrounds cross-fade over about 1 s.
- **Tests:**
  - At noon during a Dark Sun, `SkyLight` is ≤ 0.5 × a clear noon.
  - `SkyLight` is not grey during a Rust Moon.
  - `EventAtmosphere.MusicTheme` is non-null for every night, day and invasion event.
  - Play-test screenshots: 06:00, 12:00, 19:00, the Rust Moon and the Dark Sun.

### D2 · PE-6 and BO-14: music variety and per-boss music (D-55)
- An 800 ms crossfade between styles.
- A night variant per theme, using a key or mode change and a sparser groove.
- Event themes: the Rust Moon, Dark Sun, Scavenger Raid, pirates, Precursor Probes, pillars and the Colony Festival.
- Boss tiers each get a style: gate/easy, hard, event, final and astro (`AudioDirector.cs` ≈112, style rows in `AudioEngine.cs` ≈652).
- Underground variants for every biome, plus space or sky islands, the ocean and rain.
- An optional loader for `Assets/Music/<theme>.wav`, like `LoadRecordedSounds`.
- **Tests:**
  - `--music-preview` renders every new theme.
  - A self-test maps each event id and each boss tier to a theme name.
  - Play-test: walking from grassland into Frozen fades over at least 0.5 s with no click.

### D3 · PE-13: weather polish
- Rain and snow skip columns whose sky stop is above the camera (reuse `LightMap.SkyStop`), and splash on surfaces.
- Wind sway for trees, grass fringes and vines: a shear of wind × sin(t + x), drawn live for flora.
- Sandstorm and blizzard particles.
- Twinkling stars, and shooting stars during Starfall.
- Cloud parallax.
- **Tests:** play-test only. No rain streaks inside a roofed house during rain, and the trees sway on a windy day.

### D4 · PE-17: ambient sound beds
- Synthesised loops on the Ambient volume:
  - wind on the surface, scaled by `Weather.Wind`;
  - birds by day and crickets at night;
  - cave drips underground;
  - a lava rumble near uranium;
  - a waterfall near falling liquid.
- They use the mixer headroom from PE-15.
- **Tests:** `--ambient-preview` renders each bed to WAV. Play-test: the beds change from day to night and from surface to cave.

## Stream E: Grave field, dials, readouts

### E1 · EV-14: the grave field becomes a real mini-biome
- Count markers with Terraria's min, threshold and max shape to get an intensity from 0 to 1.
- The intensity drives:
  - fog, a darker sky and stars (D reads it);
  - a grave theme;
  - rare flashes (C);
  - daytime grave spawns.
- Add a `NeedsGraveField` recipe condition for 1-2 original recipes.
- **Tests:**
  - Intensity is 0 at 3 markers, between 0 and 1 at 5, and 1 at 9.
  - The grave roster spawns at noon at intensity ≥ 0.5.
  - `--craft-audit` reaches the new recipes. The audit assumes biome conditions can be met.

### E2 · EV-15: sundial and moondial analogs
- A placeable "Chrono dial" skips to dawn, and a "Lunar dial" skips to dusk.
- Each has a 7-day cooldown, which a Rust Moon or Dark Sun resets.
- They are refused while an event blocks sleep.
- Recipes go at the Fabricator Mk2 tier. The Chief Engineer also sells them.
- **Tests:** using the dial at 10:00 jumps to 06:00 the next day. A second use is refused during the cooldown.

### E3 · EV-16 and PU-17: clock, moon, weather and info-gear readouts
- Readouts:
  - weather and moon phase (`MoonPhaseName`);
  - fishing power;
  - speed in tiles/s;
  - DPS;
  - the kill tally of the last creature;
  - a rare-creature radar.
- Each readout comes from an info-gear tag (watch, sextant and weather-radio analogs, plus new info items) and has a HUD eye toggle saved in preferences.
- The always-on clock stays (a Rimtera choice).
- **Tests:** each tag adds its line. `InfoLines` holds the moon phase name with the sextant analog. A toggled-off line hides while the item stays worn.

## Release acceptance (0.1.12)

**Self-tests:** `EventScopeSelfTests`, `DefenseProtocolSelfTests`, `WeatherClimateSelfTests` and `LeisureInfoSelfTests` are registered. The moon checks in `WorldLeisureSelfTests` are updated for 15 and 20 waves. 0 FAIL.

**Audits:**

- `--item-audit`: clean.
- `--music-preview` and `--ambient-preview` render.
- `--engine-bench`: weather and sky add at most 1 ms per frame.
- Saves from 0.1.11 load. `WeatherState` migrates.

**Play-test checklist:**
1. Rain while underground: no rain creatures come down. Rain on the surface: they do.
2. A Rust Moon turns the sky red, plays its own theme, and ends at dawn.
3. A raid announces itself and marches in from an edge at dawn.
4. Defend the pylon. Let it die once: the event fails. Clear T1: you get medals and a shop.
5. Walking from snow to grassland, the snow stops but the clouds stay. Lightning flashes, and nothing hurts.
6. A windy day pushes your jumps. Kites fly.
7. A Hollow Moon reaches wave 15 with rising loot.
8. A grave field darkens and spawns by day. The dials skip time.
9. The music crossfades. Night sounds different. Each boss tier has its own theme.

## Risks
- **Music and sky are content-heavy.** Mitigation: procedural themes first. Recorded tracks are optional.
- **The global weather changes `WeatherState`.** Mitigation: append-only fields and the fixture test.
- **The invasion front interacts with NC-19 raids next step.** Mitigation: expose the front as a public API now.
