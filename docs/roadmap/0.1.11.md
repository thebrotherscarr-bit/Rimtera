## Goals
- **The pick ladder has real rungs** (D-4). Each drill opens new ore. World stones carry their gates.
- **Nothing from hardmode before the Fracture.** No viridium, void essence, tier-6 items or hardmode crates. Altar ore scales with world size and is banded by tier.
- **A Terraria-shaped world:**
  - full-width Fracture scars that convert walls (D-31);
  - crystal and blight compete for natural ground (D-30);
  - a full-height jungle that blight never eats;
  - a bigger dungeon and temple, bigger islands and grottos;
  - cave water and acid pools (D-32);
  - liquid flow rates and interactions;
  - a space band with low gravity (D-33);
  - every biome reading.
- **Growth to Terraria's ceiling** (D-1): 500 life, a vital-crystal analog, Terraria-sized luck and fishing upgrades, Lifeforce +20%.
- **Crafting shape:**
  - the Power Forge needs bars;
  - the Loom is built at the Sawmill;
  - gel torches and a hand campfire;
  - box station reach.
- **Tile rendering:** no seams, no liquid hitch, smooth liquid surfaces, per-biome cave backdrops, framing variants.

## Stream A: Ore and mining ladder

### A1 · PC-5 and WT-7: rebuild the pre-hardmode ladder (D-4)
- **Thresholds**, mapped through `DrillPowerFromTerraria` where noted:

  | Material | Threshold |
  |---|---|
  | Ferrite, scrap, cobalt | 0 |
  | Glacial, uranium | 35 |
  | Meteorite | 50 |
  | Obsidian, vitrified glass | 55 |
  | Pyrocite | 60 (Terraria hellstone, 65) |
  | Blight rock, rift soil, pearl stone | Terraria 65 equivalent |
  | Dungeon brick | Terraria 100 equivalent |
  | Viridium | 80 |
  | Lumenite / voidsteel | 85 / 100, unchanged |

- Keep the tier-3 drill spread (55, 60, 80) as the rungs. Files: `TileGrid.cs` ≈662-673 and 693, `ContentTiles.Theme.cs` ≈26, `ContentTiles.cs` ≈168, 183-184 (geodes).
- **Route check.** The Fission Tyrant path (core igniter, Irradiated Core access) must not need a drill above the Power Forge Mk2 tier. Any obsidian or pyrocite on the guaranteed route is mineable at that tier. If it is not, adjust the route with B, not the gate.
- **Tests (`MiningLadderSelfTests`):**
  - Each ore's minimum power is above the previous tier's drill and at most the drill one rung below.
  - The kit drill fails on pyrocite and viridium.
  - `--craft-audit` shows "Drill power 55, 60 and 80" milestones that each add items.
  - The Fission Tyrant is still reachable.
  - A `ParityWorldTests` table asserts the world-tile gates.

### A2 · WT-2 and PC-6: no hardmode materials before the Fracture
- **Viridium.** Delete the `ViridiumOre` call in `OreVeins` and replace it with a deep cobalt band. In `BiomeOres`, Overgrowth pockets host glacial or uranium. The altars seed viridium (D-4). Remove viridium ore from the pre-Fracture biome-chest tables.
- **Void essence before the Fracture:**
  - Depth finds and slag chests give obsidian or ancient power cells (`WorldGenContent.cs` ≈181, 192).
  - Dungeon chests give pyrocite bars or credits (`Physics.cs` ≈721).
  - Void scribes and crypt flitters drop precursor ash or credits until the Fracture (`ContentFillData.cs` ≈230-231 through the generator's drop conditions, using 0.1.9's `NpcDropDefinition` conditions).
- **X-CR-8.** The Astral crate cannot be caught before the obelisks fall (`Fishing.cs` catch table condition).
- **Tests:**
  - `WorldGenSelfTests`: Tiny and Small worlds contain 0 `ViridiumOre`, `LumeniteOre` and `VoidsteelOre`.
  - `--craft-audit --items` lists `ViridiumOre`, `ViridiumBar`, `VoidEssence`, `VoidDarts`, `VoidFuel` and `ViridiumBolts` at or after the Fracture stage.
  - Tier-6+ before the Fracture is 0.
  - **Tighten `CraftingAuditSelfTests`:** the tier-4+ before-Fracture list must be a subset of the whitelist. The whitelist is the remaining Tinker Bench combinations until PC-16 (0.1.13), plus listed fishing finds and rare drops. Name each one in `ExceptionFor`, with the reason "until PC-16".

### A3 · WT-6: altar ore scales with world size and is banded by tier
- Spots = `area × k / (1 + smashes/3)`. Pick k so that Small matches today's first-smash yield and Large gives about 4× that. Remove the cap of 24.
- Depth bands:
  - tier 1: from the cavern row to 70% of the height;
  - tier 2: from 55%;
  - tier 3: from 70% down to `moltenTop`.
- Per-world alternates are out of scope; there is no save change.
- **Tests:** the first smash on Large seeds at least 3× the cells of the first smash on Small. The mean depth of tier 3 is greater than that of tier 1.

## Stream C: Fracture, spread, liquids

### C1 · WT-3: full-width Fracture scars that convert walls (D-31)
- Arm width = `random(200, 250) × width / 4200`.
- The start column is on the dungeon side. Each arm runs diagonally to the bottom.
- Natural walls convert to radiated, overgrowth or crystal walls (`BlightRules.ConvertWall`, `CrystalRules.ConvertWall`).
- Batch the reframe and show a progress message (`GameSimulation.Blight.cs` ≈406-457, `WorldTicker.cs` ≈17-30).
- **Tests:**
  - On Small, `FractureCellsConverted + FracturePrismCells` is at least 150,000.
  - Arms are at least 180 tiles wide at the surface.
  - A beacon still shelters its radius.
  - `--engine-bench` shows the Fracture finishing in under 1 s on Large.

### C2 · WT-13: wall conversion by spread; crystal and blight compete (D-30)
- Random updates on blight convert the natural walls next to them. Purifiers revert them.
- The crystal no longer purifies blight. Each spreads only onto natural ground, as hallow and evil do in Terraria (`WorldTicker.cs` ≈179-241).
- **Tests:**
  - After 10⁶ random updates next to blight, natural walls in a 5-tile ring are converted.
  - Crystal next to blight converts no blight cell.

### C3 · WT-12: liquid behaviour parity
- Flow rates per liquid: lava and uranium move every 3rd step, nectar every 5th.
- Phase liquid touching another liquid becomes a new "phase-glass" block. Append the `TileId`; the sprite is a placeholder.
- Water evaporates below `_moltenTop`.
- Lava burns grass to soil and canopy grass to mud (`ActiveLiquidSimulator.cs` ≈62-330).
- **Tests (`FractureLiquidSelfTests`):**
  - A 10-cell lava column spreads more slowly than water.
  - Water poured into the Core empties within N steps.
  - Phase next to water hardens.

### C4 · PE-9: liquid hitch and liquid look
- Cap liquid work per tick: spread the active set over ticks with a budget of about 2 ms and carry the rest over.
- Move the live liquid pass to `GraphicalSandbox.Liquids.cs`. Draw:
  - a smoothed surface (the average of neighbour depths);
  - translucency over blocks;
  - a slow shimmer;
  - falling columns as waterfalls.
- **Tests:** in `--engine-bench`, the worst flood tick is at most 33 ms (now 794 ms) and the settled state is the same. A play-test shows no stair-steps on a lake surface.

## Stream D: Zones, space, growth

### D1 · WT-14 and PU-4: the space band (D-33)
- Player gravity multiplier = `clamp((y − topMargin) / (surfaceY × k), 0.25, 1)` in the top band, through a gravity-scale input in `MovementInput` (`PawnMovement.cs`, `GameSimulation.cs` ≈675).
- A "Low gravity" buff icon.
- `BiomeScanner` reports `Space` above about 8% of the height, for spawns and music. EV and PE pick it up in 0.1.12.
- **Tests:** at row 20 the held apex is at least 2× the surface apex. The multiplier is 1.0 at the surface. The zone reads Space.

### D2 · WT-15: the missing biome readings
- Add `BiomeZone` readings for Dungeon (precursor wall and brick), Scrap Hive, Alabaster/Basalt, Phase pool and Graveyard (`BiomeScanner.cs` ≈6-31, 91).
- **Test:** the `BiomeScanner` self-test reads each zone at a generated site of its kind.

### D3 · PC-12, IC-23 and X-CR-9: life to 500 (D-1)
- Life cores give +20 each, up to 15 cores (400). Lifebloom gives +5, up to 20 pods (500) (`Actors.cs` ≈91-95, `GrowthItems.cs` ≈41-46).
- Worldgen life-core density rises to support 15 per world: at least 15 per million cells on Small (`WorldGenDensity.cs` ≈92; B owns the file, so ask B for the one-line change).
- Old characters migrate: count cores consumed × 20, capped at the new formula, so nothing is lost.
- The Overseer patrol threshold (max HP ≥ 140) stays: it is Terraria's King Slime readiness rule.
- `full-vitality` fires at 500.
- Lifeforce gives +20% max life instead of a flat +80 (X-CR-9).
- `EnemyHitScale` and `DefenseBalance` are reviewed. Only the obvious outliers change here; the full curve is PC-15 in 0.2.0.
- **Tests (`GrowthSelfTests`):** the caps are 400 and 500. An old character with 10 cores reads 300 (100 base + 10 × 20). The achievement fires at 500.

### D4 · PC-17: permanent-upgrade parity
- Add a vital-crystal analog: an appended `ItemId` that gives permanent life regen, read by IC-1's unit regen.
- The fortune lattice gives about +0.05 luck. The bait gland gives +3 fishing.
- Every upgrade comes from post-Fracture bosses only (PC-4 in 0.1.7). The new one drops from a post-Fracture event boss.
- **Tests:** the new upgrade raises regen. All eight upgrades round-trip through the character file.

## Stream E: Tile rendering

### E1 · PE-8: remove the tile seams
- Find the source:
  - (a) dark 1-px borders in `tile_*` textures: check their edge rows;
  - (b) `TileArt.ShapeBody` drawing outlines on wrongly exposed sides;
  - (c) `PixelOffsetMode.Half` together with the lightmap blit at chunk edges.
- Fix it in code first: integer offsets, `PixelOffsetMode.None` for chunks, trimmed edges at load. Re-generating textures (X-QW-2) is optional and happens only on the user's go.
- **Test:** a scripted pixel probe on captures at 100% and 150% zoom finds no line more than 15% darker than its neighbours at tile or 480-px boundaries.

### E2 · PE-19: per-biome cave backdrops with parallax
- The cave-band wall art follows the biome: frozen ice, junk, canopy mud, fungal, crystal.
- The backdrop moves into its own pass, scrolling at about 0.85 of the camera (`GraphicalSandbox.World.cs` `DrawDepthBackdrop`, `GraphicalSandbox.Chunks.cs`).
- **Tests:** screenshots underground in Frozen and Fungal differ. The backdrop lags the terrain.

### E3 · WT-17: framing variants and cross-group merges
- A 2-bit random variant, derived from x and y and never saved, for natural blocks.
- Merge pairs so masks join across groups: soil with rock, ores, clay, mud and sand.
- Slopes count in the mask (`TileFramingSystem.cs` ≈66-100, `TileArt.cs`).
- **Tests:** `--tile-sprites` shows the variants. A soil cell next to rock has the neighbour bit.

## Risks
- **The ladder versus the Fission Tyrant route.** Mitigation: the audit is a gate on every merge, and the route check is in A1.
- **Fracture conversion cost.** Mitigation: a batched reframe, measured with `--engine-bench`.
- **Worldgen time growth.** Mitigation: per-pass timings in `--engine-bench` and a soft budget.
- **Old worlds keep old viridium.** Mitigation: documented as grandfathered. A world with the Fracture already active is unaffected.
