## Goals
- **Creature families behave like their Terraria analogs:**
  - shooters and stationary turrets fire;
  - fighters jump about 5-6 tiles, turn when stuck, drop through platforms and roam;
  - swimmers stay in water and flop on land;
  - ghosts phase through walls;
  - chargers dash;
  - climbers cling to walls;
  - splitters and summoners spawn children;
  - bats and eyes fly erratically.
- **Spawning parity:** battle, calming and peace controls; an annulus spawn area; Terraria's despawn rules; rare gold critters; critter polish.
- **Visuals and sound:** attack frames, projectile sprites, the unused player frames, hurt frames, per-creature sound families.
- **The bestiary shows stats and drops.**
- **The boss framework (D-15):**
  - data-driven patterns for all 33 bosses;
  - AI families for bosses;
  - minions and enrage;
  - the summon rules table (night, zone, per-type; D-11, D-12);
  - placement;
  - stacked bars with an aggregate API;
  - obelisk shields at 100 (D-14);
  - mini-boss patterns.

## Stream A: Ground and air AI

### A1 · EN-2: fighter pathing (D-19)
- Per-archetype `JumpPower`, default 5.5 tiles. Jump when the wall ahead is no taller than `JumpPower`. If still blocked after 1.5 s, turn around for 3 s.
- `Vertical = 1` (drop through) when the target is > 2 tiles below on a platform (`TilePhysics.IsPlatform`).
- Outside the alert radius, walk in roam legs like critters. Retune chase speeds against PU-1's 11 tiles/s player: the fighter walk speed band is 30-60% of the player's.
- Closed doors still block. Terraria's doors open only for certain enemies on Blood Moon; skip that.
- **Tests (`EnemyAiSelfTests`):**
  - A walker crosses a 3-tile wall within 6 s.
  - A walker on a platform reaches the player below within 5 s.
  - An unalerted walker's x changes by ≥ 2 tiles in 10 s.
  - A 7-tile wall holds.

### A2 · EN-13: the missing AI families
- **Ghost:** a `Phases` flag skips tile collision for non-boss fliers.
- **Charger:** run up, then dash at ≥ 2× walk speed.
- **Wall climber:** cling to solid columns and walls, and climb.
- **Splitter/summoner:** spawn N children on death or on a timer.
- **Erratic flight:** a bat jitter and an eye bounce.
- **Assignments:**
  - Ghost: Void wisp, Grave wisp, Crypt spirit.
  - Charger: Shard strider, Snow-wolf, hounds.
  - Wall climber: Burrow spider, Silk spider-mite, Venom recluse.
  - Splitter: Mite broodmother, gel kinds.
  - Tethered lunger (D-23): the stationary plants (Snapjaw and the like) hang from their anchor tile and lunge within a tether radius.
- **Tests:** one per family.
  - The ghost crosses a 5-thick wall.
  - The charger's peak speed is ≥ 2× its walk.
  - The spider climbs a 6-tile wall.
  - The broodmother spawns ≥ 2 mites.
  - The lunger never leaves its tether radius.

### A3 · EN-9: despawn parity
- Add an archetype flag `Nocturnal`. After dawn, nocturnal creatures walk away and despawn out of view.
- `DespawnSeconds` becomes ≈12 s. `DespawnDistance` becomes view-relative (≈2 views), with the IL numbers from EN-18 in 0.1.9.
- **Tests:** night spawns are gone by 08:00 if off-screen; a creature 40 tiles away for 5 s stays.

  - **Bespoke sequences** for the 11 progression bosses: Overseer, Alpha Skitter, Plant Overgrowth, Fallout Hive-mind, Scout Mech, Fission Tyrant (interim until BO-19), the hard trio, Mutated Flora, Vault Colossus, Void Cantor, Astro-God (interim until BO-20).

- **Tests (`BossPatternSelfTests`):** spawn each boss for 20 s and record a signature: pattern ids, peak speed, minions, projectile count.
  - No two of the 33 match.
  - Each boss uses ≥ 2 patterns per phase.
  - Phase 2 adds ≥ 1 new pattern.
  - Seeded for determinism; compare signature bands, not exact floats.

## Risks
- **BO-1 is the largest change in the plan.** Mitigation: land the executor with the fallback first, then migrate the bosses in batches of 5, each with signature checks.
- **Signature flakiness.** Mitigation: fixed seeds and bands.
- **Art.** Placeholders ship; the ComfyUI queue waits for the user's go.
- **Performance with many shooters.** Mitigation: projectile pooling if the bench regresses.
