# Parity C: combat, health and spawning

This file covers MECHANICS_PARITY.md section C. The probes live in `Systems/ParityCombatTests.cs` and run as part of `dotnet run -- --self-test` (wired in through `CoreSelfTests.cs`). All tuning is Rimtera's own and is collected in `CombatTuning` (`Systems/Content/CombatModel.cs`).

| # | Behaviour | Status | Notes |
|---|-----------|--------|-------|
| C1 | Damage spread, crits, crit chance | FIXED | Every weapon hit now varies by ±8% on its own RNG stream, so the legacy crit and spawn streams are not shifted. A crit doubles the varied damage. Crit chance is the weapon's chance plus its calibration plus worn crit bonuses, which now apply to every weapon. Before this change they only applied to weapons that already had a crit chance. A weapon with 0% crit and no gear still never rolls. |
| C2 | Knockback reduced by resistance; bosses resist | FIXED | `CombatTuning.KnockbackResistance` sets how much knockback each creature ignores. Critters ignore none. Heavier creatures, large creatures and worms ignore more, up to 90%. Rooted creatures ignore all of it. The gate boss ignores 85%, early and event bosses 92%, and later bosses 100%. Every knockback the player deals now goes through `KnockEnemy`: generic melee, projectiles, styled swings and thrown weapons, and explosions. |
| C3 | Per-source immunity; one swing hits once; piercing hits each once | OK | The probe confirms that a broadsword swing hits a creature once, that a projectile with pierce 3 hits a creature once, and that immunity is tracked per attacker (`MarkHitBy`/`CanBeHitBy`). Also fixed: projectile hits called the on-hit gear effects twice (double lifesteal and charge-on-hit). |
| C4 | Defense | OK | Verified. Detailed coverage is in `DefenseSelfTests`. |
| C5 | Life regeneration | FIXED | New natural regeneration. Nothing for 4 s after any loss of HP, then the rate builds over 26 s to about 1.3% of max life per second, ×1.75 when standing still. Any HP loss resets it at once, and that includes damage over time, so debuffs also pause regeneration. Gear and status regeneration is applied on top, and it also works during the delay. |
| C6 | Charge regeneration delay, still bonus, charge sickness | FIXED | Spending charge starts a 1.1 s delay, then regeneration ramps from 20% to full over 1.6 s, ×1.5 when standing still. Charge restoratives (quick charge or using the item) cause 6 s of charge sickness: Tech damage starts 25% lower and the penalty fades out with the sickness. DIFF: the sickness timer is not saved, because it lasts only seconds. |
| C7 | Quick heal picks the smallest potion that covers the hurt; potion sickness | OK | Verified. |
| C8 | Death: credit share, tombstone, longer respawn while a boss lives | FIXED | On respawn, the death site gets a pile of 40% of carried credits on Classic and all of them on Ironman. Explorer drops nothing, as its description promises. Credits are no longer part of Classic's "lose half of each material stack" rule. DIFF: Classic still halves carried material stacks, which is an existing Rimtera rule. The tombstone and the boss respawn delay were already in place. |
| C9 | Spawning: safe radius, rate, cap, night/underground/blight/events up, town down, despawn | FIXED | Spawning moved to `GameSimulation.Spawning.cs`. Spawns still land outside the 22-tile safe band and off-screen. Rate and cap multipliers: night ×1.7/×1.5, underground ×1.45/×1.35, blight ×1.3/×1.25, events unchanged (event manager). Town NPCs within 45 tiles divide the rate by (1 + 0.6n); 3 or more cut it to 12%. The cap now counts only living non-boss creatures. A creature that stays 48 or more tiles from the player for 3 s despawns. Bosses, event units, raid units, rooted creatures and mimics stay. The ambient creature-family spawner also respects the safe radius, which it could breach after being clamped at a world edge, and pauses near a populated town or while a boss lives. |
| C10 | Spawn locations | FIXED / DIFF | Surface spawns use the biome roster of the player's zone and need ground. Cave spawns now need a roofed pocket, so a cave spawn never appears under open sky. Every spawn now needs a clear body box, so flyers appear in open air and never inside terrain. DIFF: Rimtera has no water-only creatures yet, so there is no "needs liquid" rule. "Dark" means roofed; there is no light-level check. |
| C11 | Contact damage then immunity frames; critters harmless | OK | Verified. |
| C12 | Bosses leave when the player is dead; night bosses leave at dawn; spawns drop while a boss lives | FIXED | Bosses leave 3 s after the player dies. The short grace period keeps the longer boss respawn timer from C8 meaningful. Dusk event bosses (`EnemyArchetypes.EventBosses`) leave when dawn breaks; other bosses stay. While any boss is alive, the regular spawn rate is ×0.35 and the cap is ×0.6. |

**Counts:** 4 OK (C3, C4, C7, C11) and 8 FIXED (C1, C2, C5, C6, C8, C9, C10, C12). C6, C8 and C10 each have a minor DIFF, noted above.

**Superseded numbers (gear mechanics pass):** C1 now uses a ±15% whole-percent spread, a 4% base crit, Command never crits, crits double after creature defense, and knockback has soft and hard caps with a ×1.4 crit push. C5 and C6 now use the reference regeneration and charge formulas. See `docs/GEAR_DIFF_MECHANICS.md` §1, §5 and §6.
