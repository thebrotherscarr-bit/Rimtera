# Asset requests

Art and audio Rimtera needs from the owner's local ComfyUI runs (`docs/VISION.md` §7, `docs/COMFYUI.md`). The owner
decides when ComfyUI runs. Each step that adds something to see or hear lists it here.

**Style for every request:** `docs/STYLE_GUIDE.md`, which builds on
- the Rimtera style in `docs/ART_PROMPTS.md` and `docs/BIOME_THEMES.md`;
- the original sheets in `art/sheets/`;
- the boss sheet (`art/sheets/sheet_00c_bosses.jpg`: riveted gunmetal, amber-gold accents) is the preferred reference
  when a choice has to be made.

**Status:**
- *open:* not made yet; a kitbashed placeholder is in the game.
- *refine:* made, but it needs another pass.
- *done:* the real asset is in `Assets/` and the placeholder is gone.
