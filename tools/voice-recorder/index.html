Rimtera Voice Booth

Record your own sounds for the game. Clips are trimmed, levelled and saved as Assets/Sounds/<name>.wav, where the game picks them up in place of its built-in sound.

Not connected. Pick your Rimtera folder (the one with Rimtera.csproj) so clips save straight into the game.
How this works
  1. Click Connect Rimtera folder and choose your local Rimtera repo folder. (In Chrome or Edge. Other browsers download the files instead, and you move them into Assets/Sounds yourself.)
  2. Pick a sound, hit Record (or press R), make the noise, stop. Do a few takes.
  3. Tick keep on the takes you like. Keep several and the game picks one at random each time, so it doesn't get repetitive.
  4. Optional: slide pitch to make a take deeper (monsters, death) or higher (pickups, squeaks).
  5. Save, then commit and push Assets/Sounds with git.

Tips: quiet room, a hand's width from the mic, short and punchy for action sounds. Silence at the start and end is trimmed automatically.
